# Using the Advatar CRM

A short guide for the team. Nothing technical.

---

## Signing in

Go to the CRM address and sign in with your email and password. Where
you land depends on your role — you don't need to pick anything.

The **sun/moon button** in the top right switches between light and
dark. Your choice is remembered on that device.

The **magnifying glass** (or just pressing `/`) searches every client
and lead from anywhere. Start typing a name and press it.

---

## The roles

| Role | What they see |
|---|---|
| **CEO** | Everything: all clients, all money, staff payments, and who's on the team. |
| **Operations Manager** | Sales and strategy: all clients, the pipeline, prospects, and invoices. Not staff wages, not each client's monthly value, and can't invite people. |
| **Staff** | Only the clients they've been assigned to. No money at all. |
| **Videographer** | Only their assigned clients, read-only, plus their own payments. |
| **Client** | Only their own project portal. |

**Important:** a staff member genuinely cannot reach another client's
information — not by searching, not by guessing a link. That's enforced
in the database, not just hidden in the menus. To give someone access
to a client, open that client and add them under **Assigned Team**.

## Day to day

### Dashboard
Your morning screen. **Needs attention** at the top is the list to
clear: overdue invoices, overdue follow-ups, tasks due today, and
clients nobody has spoken to in three weeks. Everything on it clicks
through to the thing itself.

### Pipeline
Your leads, in three columns: **Leads** (not pitched yet), **Proposal**
(pitched, waiting), and **Recently won**.

- **Weighted pipeline** at the top is what you're likely to actually
  land — each lead's value multiplied by how likely it is. A lead with
  no likelihood set counts as 50%.
- Anything past its follow-up date is **outlined in red**.
- **Log contact** records that you spoke to them and sets the next
  follow-up in one go. Use this every time you talk to a lead — it's
  what keeps the red outlines honest.
- **→ Proposal** and **→ Won** move a lead along.

### Clients
Everyone you work with, leads included. Search and filter at the top.
**+ Add client** creates a new one — set the stage to "Lead" if they're
not signed yet.

### A client's page
- **Info** — their details, notes, and (if you're CEO) their monthly value.
- **Onboarding** — a checklist created automatically when a client
  becomes Active. Tick items off. You can add your own steps, and apply
  a **task template** to create a whole set of dated tasks at once.
- **Finance** — their invoices and what they owe. CEO and ops manager only.
- **Tasks** — day-to-day work for this client.
- **Documents** — upload briefs, contracts, brand guidelines. Files are
  private: only your team and that client can open them.
- **Meetings** — attach a Fathom recording. Paste the link and the
  summary; if they have no 90-day plan yet, one gets drafted from it.
- **90-Day Plan** tab — their content plan.
- **Activity** tab — everything that's ever happened to this client.

### Finance
Money coming in. Invoices sorted with what needs chasing at the top.

- **Draft** — you're still preparing it.
- **Sent** — it's with the client, waiting.
- **Paid** — done.
- **Overdue** — sent, and the due date has passed. Worked out
  automatically; you never set it.

**Mark sent** and **Mark paid** are one click from the list.

### Payments
Separate from Finance, and the other direction: what you pay staff and
videographers. CEO only. Staff see just their own under **My Payments**.

### This week
The last seven days on one screen: new leads, deals won, invoices
raised and paid, tasks completed, and what's overdue right now.

### Prospects
Discovery calls that Fathom sent in automatically and nobody has
reviewed yet. Open each one and check it before acting on it — nothing
in there has been confirmed by a person.

### Team
Invite a new login and set their role. CEO only. There's also a link
here to **Task templates** — repeatable sets of tasks (a shoot cycle,
an onboarding run) your team can apply to any client in one click.

## Habits that make it work

The CRM is only as good as what goes into it. Three things matter most:

1. **Log contact every time you speak to a lead.** Everything about
   chasing depends on it.
2. **Set a follow-up date whenever you leave something open.** It
   creates a task automatically, so it can't quietly disappear.
3. **Mark invoices sent and paid as it happens.** Otherwise the money
   figures — and the overdue list — are fiction.
